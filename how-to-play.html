How to play Mining Tycoon

Mine the four seasonal tokens, upgrade your rigs, time your trades and finish the round with the best score. Everything below describes the game as it works today.

Quick start

  1. Create an account or sign in on the lobby page.
  2. Pick a game in Open Games and click Enter game. The game board opens and connects by itself.
  3. Your four tokens start mining at once. Buy upgrades in the season cards to mine faster.
  4. When a trade unlocks, open Trade to move tokens into the season you want to hold.
  5. When the round (or your session) ends, a Game Over screen appears. Click it to go back to the lobby, where Last Game Highscores shows the Top 5.

The goal and the four tokens

There are four seasonal tokens: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. Every player in a round starts from the same position: 1,000 of each token and no upgrades. The round's conditions (prices, halvings and events) are fixed when the round is created and are the same for everyone.

Your goal is the best score at the end of the round. How the score is counted depends on the round's scoring mode. There is no real money in the game and you never trade with other players.

The game runs on a simulated calendar of sim-months that is compressed into the real round length. One season cycle is 36 sim-months; longer rounds play more cycles. The clock in the header (⏱) shows the real time that is left.

Mining

  • You mine all four tokens at the same time, every second. You do not have to click anything to mine.
  • Each season card shows the token's Balance, its Output per second and the time to its next Halving.
  • Output per token = base rate × hashrate × efficiency × (1 + cooling), reduced by halvings and changed by active output events.
  • The Player State panel shows output per token and in total, your balances, oracle prices, cumulative mined tokens and the conversion fee and spread.

Upgrades

Every season card has three upgrade lanes. An upgrade only improves the token of that card, and each lane has its own level per season.

Upgrade What it does (default economy)
Hashrate Raises the hashrate multiplier by 0.1 per level (starts at 1.0).
Efficiency Raises the efficiency multiplier by 0.05 per level (starts at 1.0).
Cooling Adds 0.1 per level to the cooling bonus; output is multiplied by (1 + cooling).
  • Costs rise with every level. With the default economy a level costs 50 × 1.2level of the card's token (50, 60, 72, ...). Upgrade-cost events can make upgrades cheaper or more expensive for a while.
  • Pay with any token. The Pay column chooses which balance you pay with. If you pay with a different token, the cost is converted at the current oracle prices plus the conversion fee and spread, rounded up to whole tokens. This does not use up one of your trades.
  • The lane table shows Lvl (current level), Cost (in the card's token), Out/s (extra output per second) and BEP (break-even time: how long the upgrade needs to pay for itself).
  • The server sets the final price. Upgrades are only accepted while the round runs (sync) or while your session is active (async); outside that window the buttons are disabled with a short reason.

The admin can tune the economy for new rounds. A round's values are locked when it is created, so the numbers on your board are the ones that count.

Halvings

  • A halving cuts a token's mining output in half. Each token halves once per 36-sim-month cycle.
  • Halvings are staggered 9 sim-months apart: Spring right at the start of each cycle, then Summer (month 9), Autumn (month 18) and Winter (month 27).
  • A halving also makes the token scarcer, which pushes its oracle price up.
  • The Halving line on each season card counts down the real time to that token's next halving and changes color when it is close. Player State also shows the next halving.

Oracle prices

Every token has an oracle price that the server calculates the same way for everyone. Prices change over the round:

  • One season is in favor at a time and gets a higher price; the favored season rotates every few sim-months.
  • Halvings raise a token's price because it becomes scarcer.
  • Price events push one token or all tokens up or down for a while.

Prices decide what you get in a trade, what an upgrade costs when you pay with another token, and your score in Power mode. The current prices are in the Player State panel.

Trading

  • A trade converts part of one token balance into another token at the oracle prices, minus the conversion fee and spread. Both are set per round by the admin (by default the global economy values, 2% fee + 1% spread); the round's values are shown in Player State and in the Trade tab. You trade with the game, not with other players.
  • Each round has a limited number of trades. The admin sets the count when creating the round (0 means no trading); the default depends on the round length (session length in async rounds).
  • Trades unlock at fixed times. By default the first trade unlocks after 20% of the round (or session) and the others are spread evenly over the rest. In async rounds the times count from the start of your session. A trade you do not use stays available for later.
  • The Trade tab shows trades used, when the next trade unlocks and the full schedule. The Trading status in the action bar is always visible.

Round types: sync and async

Sync round (everyone plays together)

  • The game is open for joining during a short enrollment window.
  • When the window closes, the round starts for everyone at the same time and runs for its set length. You cannot join after the start.

Async round (play when you like)

  • The round stays open for a longer period. You can enter at any time while it is open and a full session still fits into the remaining round time.
  • Entering starts your session, which has a fixed length. You only mine while your session is running.
  • Every session starts from the same baseline (1,000 of each token, no upgrades). Your best finished session is the score that counts. Your account keeps one player per round: use Rejoin in the lobby to play another session, and your best one stays on the leaderboard.
  • The header shows your session time and how much round time is left; Player State shows This session and Best this round.

Scoring modes

The admin picks one mode when creating the round. It never changes during the round, and the Scoring badge in the header shows which one applies.

Stockpile (default)
Your score is the total number of tokens you hold, all four seasons added up.
Power
Each balance is multiplied by its oracle price at the end and the results are added up, so holding the valuable tokens pays off.
Mining Time
Your balances are turned into seconds of starting-level mining for each token and added up, so tokens that mine slowly count more per token.
Efficiency
How much you mined compared with a player who never upgraded over the same time (1.0000× = no upgrades); trades and starting balances do not count.

Events

Global events are scheduled when the round is created, so every player sees the same events at the same sim-month. An event lasts a few sim-months and hits either one token or all tokens. Several events can be active at once.

Kind Effect Examples
Output Mining output up or down (between ×0.5 and ×1.5) Hashrate Festival, Energy Shock
Upgrade cost Upgrades cheaper or more expensive Component Discount, Mining Tax
Oracle price Token prices up or down Demand Surge, Market Crash
Spread Conversions (trades and cross-token payments) cost more Liquidity Crunch

The event banner above the season cards lists all active events and how long they last; ⚡ marks the values they affect.

Live tools window and tooltips

The buttons Trade, Farm, Chat and Top 5 in the action bar open a small floating window. It does not block the board: drag it by its header, resize it, and close it with the close button, Escape or a click outside.

  • Trade: convert tokens (see Trading).
  • Farm: placeholder, farming is coming later.
  • Chat: talk to the other players in the round. Chat is social only, has no effect on the game and is not saved. A small preview with an unread counter stays visible on the board. The admin can disable chat for a round; the Chat tab then says "Chat is disabled for this round."
  • Top 5: the current leaderboard.

Tooltips: hover over or focus a small i icon or a value to see an explanation or the exact number (large numbers are shortened to k, M and B). Move away or press Escape to close it.

Accounts and your name

  • You need an account to enter a game. Create one with Create account in the lobby (username, display name, email, Discord handle, optional Telegram handle and a password of at least 12 characters with upper and lower case, a digit and a special character).
  • Your display name is your name in the game and on the leaderboard. In-game names may only use letters, digits, spaces and _ - . and have at most 24 characters; other characters are replaced, and if nothing usable is left your username is used.
  • You can change your password from the lobby while signed in.
  • You can download or delete your account data from the lobby (Download my data and Delete account). Deleting is permanent; your name in past results then shows as "Deleted player".
